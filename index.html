Dong Le

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at Nanyang Technological University (NTU), Singapore, supervised by Prof. Luu Anh Tuan.

My research interests are efficient architectures, linear attention, and knowledge retrieval.

Previously, I was an AI Research Resident at Qualcomm AI Research and VinAI, working on knowledge editing and retrieval for large language models. I received my B.Eng. in Computer Science from Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology (HCMUT) in 2024.
